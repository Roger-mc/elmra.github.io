AI assessment

How can people judge whether an AI system serves them well?

A plain-language assessment guide could help people ask better questions before adopting an AI system. This is an idea under exploration, not an assessment service or certification.

Questions we want to answer

  • Who benefits, who bears the risks, and who can challenge an outcome?
  • What evidence supports the system’s claims, and where does it fail?
  • What happens to personal information, and can people choose a human alternative?

A small first step

Draft a one-page checklist and test its clarity against two public, low-risk use cases. Use public or synthetic information only.

What would count as progress?

People can explain the main limitations and decide when human review is needed. Record misunderstandings and revise the guide.

An open question

How do we make the guide useful without implying that a simple score can establish safety?

Help us improve the idea

Share a question or critique by email. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or workplace information. The mailbox must be verified before launch.
