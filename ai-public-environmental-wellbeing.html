AI for public and environmental wellbeing

Where could AI improve shared wellbeing without shifting harm elsewhere?

We are exploring uses of AI that could support public services and environmental care. Benefits must be tested against costs, energy and resource use, unequal access and potential harm. These are proposed investigations.

Questions we want to answer

  • What public or environmental need is clearly defined?
  • Could a simpler approach achieve the same result?
  • Who oversees the work, and how would unintended harm be detected?

A small first step

Compare an AI approach and a simple baseline for one public-data problem, such as explaining local environmental information. Select the dataset and evaluation before any pilot.

What would count as progress?

Report errors, accessibility, resource use and usefulness alongside any benefit. A negative result should be publishable after review.

An open question

Which small problem has reliable public data and a clear, accountable owner?

Help us improve the idea

Share a question or critique by email. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or workplace information. The mailbox must be verified before launch.
