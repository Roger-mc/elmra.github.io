Responsible workforce transition

How can organisations adopt AI while treating people fairly?

We are exploring practical ways to include workers in decisions about AI, share productivity benefits, and support people whose roles change. No pilot or employer partnership is being announced.

Questions we want to answer

  • Which tasks change, and what do affected workers say they need?
  • What training, time, support and routes to appeal are available?
  • How will benefits and burdens be measured and shared?

A small first step

Draft a transition discussion guide using a fictional organisation. Invite critique only after the wording and outreach are approved.

What would count as progress?

The guide makes responsibilities, worker participation, support options and unresolved trade-offs clear. It does not promise job protection or guaranteed outcomes.

An open question

Who should represent affected workers, and what would make participation meaningful?

Help us improve the idea

Share a question or critique by email. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or workplace information. The mailbox must be verified before launch.
